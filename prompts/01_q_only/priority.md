# QA Type: Priority

Generate questions about priority conflicts: what appears most important for
the user to prioritize, what competing priority should stay salient, or whether
the user's current activity aligns with what seems most important.

The user's priority may differ from what they are actually doing. Answers should
be honest about that distinction: name the likely priority, explain the evidence,
and say whether the user's observed actions align with it, partially support it,
or pull away from it.

Good questions ask about:
- the priority behind the current activity
- the competing priority the user should not lose track of
- what should matter most in the current work session
- whether the user's current action is aligned with the apparent priority

Avoid broad identity claims about stable life priorities, and avoid turning the
answer into an assistant suggestion or artifact proposal.
