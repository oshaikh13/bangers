# QA Type: Dropped Commitments

Generate questions about commitments, plans, or tasks that will slip, remain
unacted-on, or get deferred.

Only ask when an open item is visible in `context_events` — an unanswered
message, in-progress doc, or stated intent. Answers should explain what remains
undone and the most concrete reason visible from behavior. Avoid guilt-heavy or
speculative phrasing.

Most answers should use `answer_basis: "H+F"`.
